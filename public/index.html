Meet Captain Sam

Sam Reid has fished these waters for over 20 years and has run Bluewater Bay Charters since 2012. Expect patient help for beginners, local know-how for experienced anglers, and plenty of good stories.

Our boat, Salt Dancer, is a 32-foot sportfisher with a shaded deck, onboard toilet and full safety gear. She is surveyed and inspected every year.

What to bring

  • Hat, sunglasses and sunscreen
  • A light jacket, as it's cooler on the water
  • Food and drinks (no glass, please)
  • A bag or esky to take your catch home

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any fishing experience?

Not at all. We show you everything, from baiting a hook to landing your fish.

What if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first. If conditions aren't safe, we'll call you the day before to move your trip or give you a full refund.

Can children come?

Yes, kids aged 5 and up are welcome. We carry child-size life jackets.

How do I pay?

Once we confirm your trip by phone or email, we'll arrange a 30% deposit. The balance is paid on the day.

Can we keep our fish?

Yes, within legal size and bag limits. We'll clean and fillet your catch at the end of the trip.
